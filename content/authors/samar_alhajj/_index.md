Dr. Samar Al-Hajj is an Assistant Research Professor at the Epidemiology and Population Health Department. Dr. Hajj is the Founding Director of the MENA Program for Advanced Injury Research (MENA PAIR). Dr. Hajj is leading multiple injury research studies to assess the burden of injury morbidity and mortality in Lebanon and the Eastern Mediterranean Region.
